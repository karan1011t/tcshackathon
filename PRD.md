# Sentinel AI
## Adaptive Responsible AI Guardrail Platform

---

# Product Vision

Sentinel AI is a **Universal Responsible AI Middleware Platform** that sits between any AI chatbot and its users, ensuring every AI-generated response is safe, reliable, explainable, and compliant before reaching the end user.

Unlike traditional moderation systems that rely on static rules, Sentinel AI introduces **Adaptive Guardrails** that continuously evolve through configurable organizational policies and developer feedback while remaining fully explainable.

The platform is designed to work with **any LLM** including Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Llama, Mistral, and enterprise AI systems without requiring modifications to the chatbot itself.

---

# Problem Statement

Large Language Models (LLMs) are powerful but unpredictable.

They can unintentionally generate:

- Unsafe or harmful content
- Biased or discriminatory responses
- Confidential information leakage
- Hallucinated information
- Unsupported factual claims
- Responses violating organization-specific policies

Current moderation systems are mostly static and difficult to customize for different industries.

A hospital, a bank, and a university all require different Responsible AI policies, yet most existing guardrail solutions apply the same fixed safety checks to every chatbot.

Organizations need an intelligent, configurable, and scalable Responsible AI layer that adapts to their business requirements without changing their chatbot.

---

# Proposed Solution

Sentinel AI acts as a **middleware safety layer** placed between the chatbot and the user.

```
                    User
                      │
                      ▼
              AI Chatbot / LLM
                      │
                      ▼
        Sentinel AI Guardrail Platform
                      │
                      ▼
            Safe AI Response
                      │
                      ▼
                    User
```

Every response generated by the chatbot is intercepted and evaluated before reaching the user.

Instead of trusting the chatbot blindly, Sentinel AI validates every response using multiple Responsible AI Guardrails.

---

# Product Philosophy

Instead of asking

> "Is this response safe?"

Sentinel AI asks

> "Does this response satisfy every Responsible AI policy required by this organization?"

Safety becomes configurable instead of hardcoded.

---

# Objectives

- Build a universal Responsible AI Guardrail Platform.
- Detect unsafe chatbot responses.
- Provide explainable risk analysis.
- Rewrite unsafe responses into safer alternatives.
- Allow organizations to customize their own guardrails.
- Continuously improve through feedback-driven policy evolution.
- Remain lightweight and scalable across industries.

# Key Features

## 1. Universal Guardrail Engine

Every chatbot response passes through multiple independent guardrails.

Core Guardrails

- Unsafe Content Detection
- Bias & Fairness Detection
- Confidential Information Leakage
- Hallucination Detection
- Unsupported Claim Detection

Each guardrail independently evaluates the response.

Output

- Pass / Fail
- Risk Score
- Confidence
- Explanation
- Highlighted Phrase

---

## 2. Adaptive Risk Engine

Instead of assigning arbitrary risk values, Sentinel AI calculates an explainable cumulative risk score.

Example

| Guardrail | Weight |
|------------|---------|
| Unsafe Content | 40 |
| Confidential Leakage | 35 |
| Bias | 20 |
| Hallucination | 15 |
| Unsupported Claims | 10 |

The final score represents the overall safety level of the chatbot response.

---

## 3. Explainability Engine

Every decision is transparent.

The platform explains:

- Which guardrail failed
- Why it failed
- Which text triggered it
- Severity level
- Suggested remediation

No black-box moderation.

---

## 4. Safe Response Generator

Instead of simply rejecting unsafe responses,

Sentinel AI automatically generates

- safer wording
- compliant responses
- developer suggestions

Example

Original

```
John's password is abc123.
```

Safe Version

```
I cannot disclose another person's credentials.
```

---

## 5. Organization Policy Manager ⭐

Every organization defines Responsible AI differently.

Sentinel AI allows administrators to create custom guardrails without writing code.

Organizations can

- Add Guardrails
- Remove Guardrails
- Edit Guardrails
- Change Severity
- Enable / Disable Policies

Examples

Healthcare

```
Never diagnose diseases.
```

Finance

```
Never recommend investments without disclaimer.
```

HR

```
Never reveal employee salaries.
```

Education

```
Maintain age-appropriate responses.
```

These policies become active immediately without modifying the chatbot.

## 7. Context-Aware Guardrails

Most guardrails evaluate a single message.

Sentinel AI evaluates the entire conversation.

Example

```
Conversation

↓

Safe

↓

Borderline

↓

Repeated Prompt Injection

↓

Risk Score Increases

↓

Guardrails Tighten
```

This enables detection of evolving attacks that appear safe individually but become dangerous over multiple turns.

---

## 8. Adaptive Policy Evolution ⭐

Unlike static moderation systems,

Sentinel AI continuously improves using developer and administrator feedback.

Workflow

```
Conversation

↓

Guardrail Trigger

↓

Developer Feedback

↓

Policy Suggestion

↓

Administrator Approval

↓

Updated Guardrail

↓

Future Conversations
```

The platform does **not retrain the LLM**.

Instead, it continuously evolves its policy layer.
